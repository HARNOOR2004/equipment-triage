## [CONV-1.1] Normal operating limits
Winding temperature: warning at 80 C, critical at 100 C. Running current: warning at 28 A, critical at 35 A. Vibration: warning at 4.0 mm/s, critical at 6.5 mm/s. Sustained operation above critical limits risks winding insulation failure.

## [CONV-2.1] Fault: motor overheating
Possible causes: overload from jammed belt or excess material, blocked cooling fan or vents, bearing friction, supply voltage imbalance, frequent start-stop cycles.

## [CONV-2.2] Fault: high running current
Possible causes: mechanical overload, belt or roller seizure, phase imbalance, worn bearings, failing gearbox. Compare current against load condition at the time of the event.

## [CONV-2.3] Fault: abnormal vibration or noise
Possible causes: bearing wear, pulley or coupling misalignment, loose mounting bolts, damaged gearbox gears, unbalanced rollers.

## [CONV-3.1] Inspection steps
1. Lock out and tag out the drive. 2. Check belt, rollers and pulleys for jamming or seizure. 3. Clear cooling vents and inspect the fan. 4. Check coupling and mounting bolts. 5. Inspect bearings for play or noise. 6. Measure supply voltage across phases.

## [CONV-3.2] Maintenance priority guidance
Critical: temperature or current at or above critical limit, or burning smell. High: warning-level readings on two or more parameters. Medium: single warning-level reading. Low: normal readings. Missing or conflicting readings must be verified before lowering priority.
