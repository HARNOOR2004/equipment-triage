## [COMP-1.1] Normal operating limits
Discharge temperature: warning at 95 C, critical at 110 C. Discharge pressure: warning at 9.5 bar, critical at 11 bar. Vibration: warning at 5.0 mm/s, critical at 8.0 mm/s. Operation above critical limits requires shutdown.

## [COMP-2.1] Fault: high discharge temperature
Possible causes: clogged oil cooler or air filter, low oil level, failed cooling fan, high ambient temperature, worn oil separator. Check oil level and cooler airflow first.

## [COMP-2.2] Fault: pressure not building or unstable
Possible causes: inlet valve malfunction, leaking discharge piping, clogged air filter, worn compressor element, faulty unloader or pressure regulator.

## [COMP-2.3] Fault: excessive vibration
Possible causes: loose mounting, worn bearings, coupling misalignment, damaged anti-vibration mounts, internal rotor wear.

## [COMP-3.1] Inspection steps
1. Lock out and depressurize before opening any component. 2. Check oil level and condition. 3. Inspect air filter and oil cooler for blockage. 4. Verify cooling fan operation. 5. Check discharge piping and fittings for leaks. 6. Inspect mounts and coupling alignment.

## [COMP-3.2] Maintenance priority guidance
Critical: temperature or pressure at or above critical limit. High: warning-level readings on two or more parameters. Medium: single warning-level reading. Low: normal readings with minor observations. Unverified or conflicting readings must be checked before downgrading priority.
